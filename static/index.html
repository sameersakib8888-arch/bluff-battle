THE RULEBOOK

Lie well.
Score big.

  1. 01
    Make up a lie

    Everyone gets a strange trivia question. You have up to 35 seconds to invent a believable fake answer.

  2. 02
    Find the truth

    The actual answer gets mixed in with everyone's lies. Vote in 25 seconds. You can't choose your own bluff.

  3. 03
    Collect points

    Correct vote: +1,000. Each opponent fooled by your lie: +500. Both can score in one round.

  4. 04
    Win the room

    Eight rounds. Highest total wins; tied top scores share the crown. Two to eight players can join.
